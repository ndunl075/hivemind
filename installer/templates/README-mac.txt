Hivemind setup (Mac)

1. Double-click the zip to unpack it. Move the Hivemind folder to your Documents or Desktop and open it.
2. Double-click "Hivemind Setup.command".
   This build is not signed yet, so macOS will refuse the first time. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down to the message about "Hivemind Setup.command", click "Open Anyway", and confirm.
   (On macOS 14 and earlier you can Control-click the file, choose Open, then Open again.)
3. Drag your project folder onto the window, press Enter, and follow the questions.
   You need the server address and your personal token from whoever runs your team's Hivemind.

You do not need Node or any other tool installed. When setup is done you can delete this folder.
